## Estimands and decisions

Carry both arms together. For each independently sampled unit i, retain its fixed number of cases mᵢ, checked decisions, labelled faults and detections, labelled nonfaults and net paired alarms. Combine IDs into the known independent template block when dependence crosses IDs. For each endpoint use the case-weighted ratio r = ΣE[Xᵢ] / ΣE[Yᵢ], estimated by ΣXᵢ / ΣYᵢ. This preserves unequal case counts. An unknown template partition or outcome-dependent cluster size requires separate evidence before applying this analysis.

Use one-sided α = .05. Issue all three bounds together. Require L(coverage) ≥ .80, L(labelled all-fault sensitivity) ≥ .80 and L(asserted FAR − checked FAR) > .05. A single failure prevents success. Intersection–union testing controls the probability of joint false success at α if each component test controls its own null at α. It requires no independence between endpoints. Simultaneous interval coverage is a separate diagnostic.

Both amendments require at least 30 independent contributing units for each labelled truth denominator. A zero denominator or fewer contributing units returns insufficient information. Missing truth stays outside its corresponding ratio, while every case stays in coverage. To interpret unlabelled cases, retain the extreme assignments specified in v3 and document possible informative missingness. A missing arm output cannot be silently turned into UNKNOWN or INVALID_TEST. [Frozen protocol](B_PROTOCOL_V3_20261121.md)

## Candidates

| Procedure | Independent unit and construction | Boundary behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Frozen v3 percentile | 10,000 paired physical-ID resamples, seed 20261121, 500th ordered value. Its original physical-ID unit is retained even in the template stress test. | Any undefined replicate, constant bootstrap distribution or insufficient ID count suppresses all three bounds. |
| Cluster t ratio inversion | Invert the one-sided studentized estimating equation for X−rY across physical IDs or independent template groups. | Decline if denominator separation fails or any ratio has constant observed residuals. |
| Bounded cluster fallback | Invert a one-sided Hoeffding test using the known maximum range of each independent group. Run as a separately prespecified procedure on every cohort. | Gives a finite conservative limit even with unanimous decisions or zero observed discordance when enough groups contribute. No selection between procedures after viewing outcomes. |

For G independent clusters and Zᵢ(r) = Xᵢ − rYᵢ, the cluster t lower root solves ΣZᵢ = t(.95,G−1)√G s(Z), using sample standard deviation. Require ΣYᵢ > t√G s(Y) so the score is decreasing and the lower root uniquely defined. Intersect the bound with the known lower support (0 for the two rates and for monotone D4 discordance, −1 for a general signed difference). Student reference is exact under the appropriate normal model and approximate for skewed discrete cluster scores. Clustering at the correct level addresses dependence but does not ensure calibration at sparse or near-boundary counts. [Fieller (1954)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1954.tb00159.x) · [Klerk-Grobben, original derivation](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/8717/8717D.pdf)

For independent bounded clusters, the range of Zᵢ(r) is at most w mᵢ. For a binary contribution 0 ≤ Xᵢ ≤ Yᵢ, w = 1. A paired signed alarm difference has w = 2, reduced to 1 only when the audited alarm ordering proves 0 ≤ Xᵢ ≤ Yᵢ on every case. At the true r, ΣE[Zᵢ(r)] = 0. With probability at least .95 the sum is at most h = w√(log(20)Σmᵢ²/2). The lower limit is (ΣXᵢ − h)/ΣYᵢ clipped to its lower support. With equal independent group sizes and 200 all-decided groups the coverage limit is 1−√(log(20)/400) ≈ .9135. The finite-sample guarantee concerns erroneous **issuance** or false success over all attempts. Conditioning on passing a random 30-contributor guard can change conditional coverage. Predeclared capacities, independent groups, valid pairing and the declared support are required. [Hoeffding (1963), Theorem 2](https://www.cs.rpi.edu/academics/courses/spring06/random/hoefding.pdf)

**Bootstrap-t disposition.** The resampled pivot divides by a bootstrap standard error. At unanimous coverage, unanimous observed sensitivity or no net-discordance variation this standard error can be zero, including inside a resample of a nonconstant dataset. Dropping those draws or substituting a constant would define a different test needing its own validation. P5B did not instantiate this fourth procedure or use an experimental fallback chosen after seeing a result. [DiCiccio & Efron (1996)](https://doi.org/10.1214/ss/1032280214)

## Evaluation and disposition

Population targets are computed from the full paired joint law **before** interpreting rejection rates. Calibration and held-out data seeds are distinct. No adjustment, seed selection, threshold change or model selection is performed on held-out results. Gates combine 80% joint power, 5% component and composite type I error and 95% conditional marginal coverage using prespecified pointwise Wilson intervals. Details and denominators are in the manifest and matrix. The all-null corner alone cannot establish intersection–union control. None of the three procedures qualifies in either phase. No v4 adoption, scientific lock or increase to the resource ceilings follows. [Decision and limitations](P5B_STATISTICAL_CALIBRATION_VI.md)
