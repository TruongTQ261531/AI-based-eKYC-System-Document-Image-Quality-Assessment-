## Candidate contribution and falsification

Proposed positioning: **When a Test Oracle Cannot Trust Its Reference: Characterizing Evidence Validation and Abstention in AI Document Processing.** Not a new OCR, MRZ parser, reject-option theory, or general proof of superiority. Candidate incremental contribution: a narrowly controlled oracle-layer intervention on an identical reader output and reference payload, testing asserted versus literal-checked MRZ evidence and explicitly separating reference eligibility from independent output-fault truth. A source-preserving/removing redundant-field case is relevant only if support and semantic precision are actually verified. This conjunction is a plausible gap in screened sources, not a verified first-ever claim.

Current evidence: D4 72 authored controls, 8 divergent controls at each tier (6 FAULT and 2 NO_FAULT withdrawn to UNKNOWN). Frozen mechanism yields no binary correction: for same admitted inputs, checked binary agrees with asserted. FAR reduction is withdrawn false alarms / labeled nonfaults; lost true detections reduce sensitivity. A shared wrong reference can fool both. This is a mechanism explanation, not a statistical or empirical generalization. P5B simulated infeasibility under selected design does not show universal impossibility.

Reviewer challenges: (1) Is literal validation simply standard input validation? Need demonstrate nontrivial oracle interpretation and documented real failure modes, not just a wrapper. (2) Is abstention merely fewer decisions? Report coverage and all-fault sensitivity together, with known/missing truth. (3) Does geometric masking really remove primary evidence and retain backup? Independent source adjudication needed. (4) Do two arms use identical information and frozen outputs? Verify shared payload and exact reader/config. (5) Does the small enriched panel generalize? No; 37 candidates/18 IDs/three templates are bounded descriptive evidence, not independent population estimates. (6) Are existing recent answerability evaluations closer than assumed? Full-text follow-up including contrastive evidence sufficiency and direct MRZ literature required.
