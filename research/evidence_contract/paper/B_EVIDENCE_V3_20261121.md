# Exposure, abstention and the D4 comparator · Evidence ledger v3

**Scope, 21 November 2026.** The supported contribution is a replayable, document-specific test of what happens when the oracle trusts declared MRZ metadata. The evidence establishes software behaviour on constructed controls, abstention on historical outputs, and limits on the available corpus. Comparative effectiveness on independently labelled prospective documents remains unmeasured. This ledger covers the claims carried forward into the v3 protocol. The [earlier B01–B12 ledger](B_CLAIM_TO_EVIDENCE_20261117.json) retains its dated workflow, planning and negative results.

## Comparison with primary literature

We examined original studies that address the nearest methodological questions in the project's [earlier evidence map](../METHODS_EVIDENCE_MAP_20261116.md), with targeted searches for metamorphic classifiers, oracle quality, risk–coverage, document grounding and duplicate-image evaluation. This is a targeted comparison. The available studies do not establish a priority claim.

## Threats and their impact on interpretation

- **Internal validity.** An oracle and its reference can inherit the same mistake. Hash binding and replay establish identity and software behaviour. For a prospective verdict, collect reference and output truth separately under blinding and log every intervention before arm evaluation. Retain correctly bound but semantically wrong controls.
- **Construct validity.** A valid checksum or matching string does not resolve a full-date versus YYMMDD mismatch. Separate semantic errors from parser usability. Count UNKNOWN and INVALID_TEST in all-case coverage, and keep unlabeled results in the enrolled frame. Report missing-truth extrema alongside estimates on labelled faults and nonfaults.
- **External validity.** Shared layouts, capture ancestry and cross-collection reuse weaken independence. The 362 dHash components need inspection. Pixel inequality does not certify novelty. Historical exposure is confirmed for 192/1,000 MIDV-2020 instances, with 808 unknown. Keep the v3 inference conditional on its prespecified defect panel and declared rights.
- **Conclusion validity.** Additional fields and raters do not add physical IDs. Resample every comparison and both arms of a document together. Sparse faults, clustered templates, informative missingness and small budgets can defeat the design. Quantify uncertainty and check operating characteristics before a scientific lock. A single-machine control timer measures neither end-to-end latency nor reviewer effort.
